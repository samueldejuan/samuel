How reserving works

Seats are limited so everyone gets a word in. Here's all there is to it.

  1. Save a seat

    Fill in the form above with your name and email. Bring a friend if you like, one guest per member.

  2. Check your inbox

    You'll get a confirmation with a calendar invite and the discussion questions about a week before.

  3. Read and come along

    Turn up a few minutes early to grab a seat. Didn't finish the book? Come anyway.

Questions

If it's not answered here, reply to any club email.

Do I need to be a member?

No. Anyone can reserve a seat. Reserving is what makes you a member, and there are no dues.

What if I haven't finished the book?

Come anyway. We don't police page counts, and the conversation often makes people want to finish.

What if the meeting is full?

The form closes when seats run out. Cancellations reopen seats, so check back the week of the meeting.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes. The back room is step-free with an accessible restroom on the same floor.
